Privacy Policy

WFHB Sunday Recorder

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Purpose

WFHB Sunday Recorder is a personal desktop utility that records scheduled radio broadcasts, processes audio files, and uploads recordings to the application owner's Google Drive account.

Information Accessed

The application uses Google OAuth authorization and requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and manage files it uploads or that are authorized for use with the application.

Use of Information

Google Drive access is used to upload, organize, and retrieve radio recordings, playlists, and associated metadata. The application is intended for personal use.

Storage and Security

Audio recordings and related files are stored locally on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored locally for authorized access. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Recordings may be shared using Google Drive links configured by the owner.
